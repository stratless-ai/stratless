# HUMAN.md

Every repo has an `AGENTS.md`. It tells your AI about your **code**: your stack, your conventions, this repo. Nothing tells it about **you**. That file is empty. stratless writes it.

`HUMAN.md` is the person layer. It lives at `~/.claude/HUMAN.md`, and your assistant loads it at the start of every session, so it starts already knowing who it's talking to instead of a blank.

## What's in it

Not a rules sheet you wrote. A short model of a person, reasoned from your real history:

- what you **know** and don't (fluent, or still learning, and the tell)
- what actually **stalls** you (the tech, or meaning and altitude)
- how you **think and talk** (orders, or reasoning out loud)
- what you're **building**, and why
- your **failure signal**: the exact moves that mean the assistant just went over your head

## profile and read

Same evidence, two readings for two readers:

```
stratless profile          the AI's copy, what loads into its context
stratless profile --read   your copy, written for you to read
```

`profile` is what your assistant reads. `profile --read` is the mirror you read: where it talked over your head, where you went quiet, and how it's trending. It is written only when you ask, over the exact evidence your loaded profile saw.

## A sample

```
who you are working with, loaded into your assistant every session

WHAT THEY KNOW
Holds the system's architecture in mind, restating a proposed
design accurately before moving on.

HOW THEY WORK
A settled recommendation gets a terse "go." They drive the
release pipeline hands-on and personally check a live artifact
against your status report rather than trust it.

FAILURE SIGNALS
Catches gaps in your status claims (an unpublished page, a
missed release note) and stops to demand verification.
```

It sharpens every time it runs.
