# HUMAN.md

Every repo has an `AGENTS.md`. It tells your AI about your **code**: your stack, your conventions, this repo. Nothing tells it about **you**. That file is empty. stratless writes it.

`HUMAN.md` is the person layer. It lives at `~/.claude/HUMAN.md`, and your assistant loads it at the start of every session, so it starts already knowing who it's talking to instead of a blank.

## What's in it

Not a rules sheet you wrote. A model of how you actually work, derived from your real history and nothing you declared:

- the **recurring things you do**, each with the count behind it
- **when something has gone wrong**: the moves that cluster around the assistant getting it wrong, so it learns your failure signals
- **how you work**: the way you think and direct, which is not friction and should not be smoothed away
- **your shorthand, decoded**: the phrases you reach for, and what you actually want when you use them

## Seeing it

```
stratless profile
```

`profile` prints the current model of you, free and instant. It is the same `HUMAN.md` your assistant loads; `profile` just shows it to you, and never spends. Building and loading it is `update`'s job. See [Commands](/docs/commands).

## A sample

Every line is observed and carries the count behind it. It leads with what tends to go wrong, so your assistant reads the signals first, then how you simply work:

```
## When something has gone wrong

Insists on a plan, and plan mode, before any code gets written.
256 times across 66 conversations
> wait. are you in plan mode?

## How they work

Gives a short signal (go, commit it) to authorize the next step.
727 times across 92 conversations
> Go with A, fix the counts
```

It sharpens every time it runs.
